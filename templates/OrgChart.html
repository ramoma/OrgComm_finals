Organizational Chart

This Matrix Organizational Structure is used mainly for the reason of skill exchange across different people with different strengths. A Matrix Structure organizes our 4-person team by instersecting functional specialties with shared project responsibilities.

This organizational structure was chosen because its decentralized, cross-functional grid mirrors real world Agile tech teams and directly embodies Douglas Mcgregor’s Theory by replacing rigid top-down control with mutual trust, autonomy, and lateral communication between your design and engineering pods.

About

VOLB is a Tech & Human Systems Lab that combines software development with editorial design. We are guided by McGregor's Theory Y, built on autonomy, trust and teamwork. VOLB stands for Villarta, Ong, Lingatong and Baltazar, the creators of this website.
