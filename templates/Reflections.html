Individual Reflections

Organizational Communication explores how information, culture, and relationships are structured within an organization to achieve shared goals. It is vital because clear communication aligns diverse teams, prevents friction, and directly shapes organizational performance and morale. By examining frameworks like McGregor’s Theory Y, I realized that modern workplaces thrive on mutual trust and lateral collaboration rather than rigid top-down command chains. I learned how to analyze team dynamics, design adaptive matrix structures, and bridge distinct domain perspectives such as technical logic and visual storytelling. Ultimately, OrgComm taught me that an organizationa’s structural design is itself a powerful form of communication.

As a Computer Science student, I thought that being a good coder means you just code well. Until the subject named Organizational Communication came up and kind of wrecked the whole idea for me, that being good at coding isn't enough If I can't communicate well with my peers. Most of the groupworks I've done had many problems that weren't really about the code because the real reason was either they just didn't understand the task, they left the message on seen that they forgot about finishing it, or nobody said anything 'till the deadline came close. Thanks to this subject, I realized that it is essential to communicate in teams and I get to apply these lessons to my daily life. The one lesson that really stuck in my mind was the process of communication which matters a lot because it shows why the message will either succeed or fail and I get to use this on my day to day basis.

Organizational Communication as a subject was something I initially glossed over when looking through my first semester schedule for the school year. I had never given that much thought about how important it would be as a way to reflect on ways I fail as a peer to my fellow students. This project alone helped me apply concepts and frameworks, especially with the case of our chosen theorist. This subject helped me tighten my grasp on how to collaborate with other people within the context of an organization, in this case our classroom, effectively.

About

VOLB is a Tech & Human Systems Lab that combines software development with editorial design. We are guided by McGregor's Theory Y, built on autonomy, trust and teamwork. VOLB stands for Villarta, Ong, Lingatong and Baltazar, the creators of this website.
